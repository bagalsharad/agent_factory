Staff Data Platform Engineer — AI Reliability
Example Corp (fictional) · Remote (US)

About the role
Our data platform team is building AI agents that diagnose and remediate pipeline failures. You will design
the platform that runs these agents safely: tool permissions, human approvals, evaluation and observability,
on top of a lakehouse that serves 400+ pipelines.

What you will do
- Design and own the architecture of our pipeline reliability platform (Spark, Airflow, Kafka, Delta Lake on AWS).
- Build agent workflows that read logs and metadata, propose fixes and request human approval for risky actions.
- Define service-level objectives for data freshness and quality; lead incident reviews.
- Build evaluation suites for agents: regression tests, prompt-injection tests, failure injection.
- Partner with security on least-privilege access, audit logging and data classification.
- Mentor senior engineers and lead cross-team technical design reviews.

What you bring
- 7+ years in data engineering, including large-scale Spark and Airflow in production.
- Experience designing streaming systems (Kafka, CDC) and lakehouse storage (Delta Lake or Iceberg).
- Hands-on experience with LLM applications or agents (tool calling, structured output, evaluation).
- Strong incident management and observability practice (SLOs, OpenTelemetry, runbooks).
- Infrastructure as code (Terraform) and CI/CD.
- Clear written communication; experience influencing without authority.

Nice to have
- Kubernetes, LangGraph or similar agent frameworks, Python packaging, cost optimisation on AWS.
