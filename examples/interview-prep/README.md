# Interview Prep

Resume + job description (+ optional previous interview feedback and videos) → resume tailored to the job as
`resume.docx` and `resume.pdf`, scenario-based questions with suggested answers, talking points from the videos,
and a study checklist with a day-by-day plan (`prep-guide.md`).
Usage: [docs/user-guide/interview-prep.md](../../docs/user-guide/interview-prep.md).

`samples/` holds a fictional candidate (`sample-resume.md`), job (`sample-jd.md`) and interview feedback
(`sample-feedback.md`), used by the evaluation suite as `fixture:sample-resume`, `fixture:sample-jd` and
`fixture:sample-feedback`.
