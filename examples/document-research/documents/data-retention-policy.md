# Data Retention Policy (Sample)

Version 3.2 — owned by the Data Governance Council.

## Execution logs
Pipeline execution logs are retained for 90 days in hot storage and then deleted automatically.
Logs that are attached to an open incident are retained until the incident is closed plus 30 days.

## Deletion approvals
Deletion of production datasets requires approval from a data owner and a member of the Data Governance Council.
Emergency deletions must be recorded in the audit log within 24 hours.

## Personal data
Customer personal data is retained for 24 months after the last customer interaction unless a legal hold applies.
